# Escalation Policy

## 1. Escalation triggers

A department must escalate when:

- target semantics are unclear
- evidence is insufficient
- responsibility boundary is unclear
- departments conflict
- critical responsibility operation is involved
- contract-protected or global danger zones are touched
- the current layer cannot close the reasoning loop

## 2. Master actions

The Master may:

- request evidence
- restart task admission
- downgrade to investigation
- ask a department to redo work
- reject the task
- accept a candidate and continue
