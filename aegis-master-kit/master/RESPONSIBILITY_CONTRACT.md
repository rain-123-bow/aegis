# Responsibility Contract

## 1. Bottom line

All critical responsibility nodes must be owned by the developer. AI must not assume critical responsibility or perform critical responsibility operations.

## 2. Critical responsibility operations

At minimum:

- final task definition confirmation
- final technical route confirmation
- formal external conclusion confirmation
- remote push
- main branch merge
- release / deployment trigger
- relaxing critical constraints
- accepting high-risk unclosed results

## 3. Master refusal rights

The Master must refuse:

- direct main-branch submission
- remote push on behalf of the developer
- final sign-off on behalf of the developer
- skipping developer review
- packaging unclosed candidates as formal conclusions
- any attempt to transfer final responsibility to AI

## 4. Governance vs responsibility

The Master owns internal rational governance.
The developer owns real-world final signing responsibility.
