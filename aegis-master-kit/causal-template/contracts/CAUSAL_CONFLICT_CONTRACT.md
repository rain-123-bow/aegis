# Causal Conflict Contract

## Canonical no-conflict rule

No two active causal claims may conflict under the same:

- scope
- version context
- valid conditions
- assumptions
- Knowledge baseline
- Causal baseline

## Conflict meaning

A conflict usually indicates:

- a reasoning error
- missing or incorrect scope
- different assumptions not declared
- stale branch
- inconsistent Knowledge baseline
- insufficient evidence
- evidence interpretation error

## Where conflicts may exist

Conflicts may exist in:

- proposals
- review records
- branch-local causal deltas

Conflicts must not remain unresolved in the merged canonical active causal baseline.

## Conflict resolution actions

- reject one proposal
- split scope
- update assumptions
- request more evidence
- invalidate old claim
- supersede old claim
- mark branch stale
- correct Knowledge baseline

## Conflict record

Conflict records must preserve what conflicted, why it was a conflict, how it was resolved, and what status transitions were made.
