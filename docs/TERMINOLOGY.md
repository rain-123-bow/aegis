# Terminology

## Master

The top-level AI governance agent that directly interacts with the developer.

## Department

A top-level organizational unit created by the Master. The Master sees the department interface, not its internals.

## Department leader

The agent responsible for a department-local structure.

## Router

A local communication domain owned by a hub. It routes messages between registered agents.

## Claim

A developer statement that has not yet been verified. A claim is not a system fact.

## Business instance

A concrete work target composed of code plus three libraries: archive, causal, and knowledge.
